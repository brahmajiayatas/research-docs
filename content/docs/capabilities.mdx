---
title: Core capabilities
description: Thirty product capabilities spanning onboarding, financial disclosure, parenting plans, AI-assisted mediation, documents and filing, professional oversight, and post-divorce management.
---

The platform’s product vision is translated into the following core capabilities, creating a structured end-to-end journey from initial onboarding and information gathering through AI-assisted mediation, professional escalation, settlement, filing preparation, and post-divorce management.

| # | Product Capability | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guided Divorce Onboarding | Guides each spouse through structured questions, explains processes, establishes expectations, and identifies required information before beginning mediation activities. |
| 2 | Case Creation | Creates a secure digital divorce case containing participants, jurisdiction, marital information, children, issues, documents, tasks, and settlement status. |
| 3 | Spouse Invitation | Allows one spouse to securely invite the other spouse, establish independent access, and begin separate information collection processes. |
| 4 | Identity Verification | Verifies participant identity using secure authentication and verification procedures before allowing access to confidential case information or communications. |
| 5 | Conflict and Safety Screening | Screens for domestic violence, coercion, intimidation, financial control, threats, substance concerns, and other circumstances requiring professional intervention immediately. |
| 6 | Financial Disclosure | Collects structured financial information, supporting documents, income records, liabilities, accounts, investments, and disclosures from both spouses independently. |
| 7 | Asset and Debt Inventory | Creates comprehensive inventories of marital and separate assets, debts, ownership, values, balances, documentation, and proposed classification. |
| 8 | Income and Expense Collection | Collects recurring income, household expenses, childcare costs, insurance, taxes, transportation, healthcare, and lifestyle information for financial planning. |
| 9 | Property Division | Helps spouses organize property, identify ownership classifications, compare proposed allocations, calculate balancing payments, and document mutually accepted division terms. |
| 10 | Parenting Plan Creation | Guides parents through custody schedules, holidays, transportation, communication, decision-making, education, healthcare, vacations, and other parenting responsibilities. |
| 11 | Child-Related Expense Planning | Organizes childcare, education, healthcare, activities, insurance, transportation, and extraordinary expenses with configurable allocation scenarios between parents. |
| 12 | Support Calculation Assistance | Provides jurisdiction-specific calculation assistance using configured legal rules, disclosed financial information, assumptions, and clearly identified calculation limitations. |
| 13 | Spousal Support Scenario Modeling | Enables users to explore hypothetical support scenarios using income, duration, assumptions, and jurisdictional rules without presenting outcomes as legal advice. |
| 14 | Negotiation Assistance | Helps spouses communicate positions, identify interests, formulate alternatives, and evaluate proposals while maintaining neutral language and avoiding coercive negotiation. |
| 15 | AI-Assisted Mediation | Uses controlled AI to summarize issues, clarify positions, generate neutral questions, identify gaps, and facilitate structured settlement discussions safely. |
| 16 | Dispute Identification | Detects unresolved disagreements by comparing responses, proposals, documents, and stated positions across financial, parenting, property, and support issues. |
| 17 | Settlement Proposal Generation | Generates structured settlement proposals from mutually accepted information, unresolved issues, user preferences, and configurable mediation rules for review. |
| 18 | Agreement Comparison | Compares spouse proposals and draft agreements, highlighting differences, missing provisions, inconsistent values, unresolved terms, and potential review requirements. |
| 19 | Document Generation | Generates organized case summaries, mediation worksheets, financial schedules, parenting plans, settlement drafts, and filing-preparation documents using approved templates. |
| 20 | Document Review | Reviews uploaded documents for completeness, inconsistencies, missing information, conflicting terms, unclear language, and issues requiring professional review. |
| 21 | Case Timeline | Maintains chronological records of invitations, disclosures, negotiations, proposals, documents, decisions, reviews, signatures, deadlines, and significant case events. |
| 22 | Tasks and Deadlines | Creates personalized tasks, reminders, required documents, review activities, mediation steps, signatures, and jurisdiction-specific filing preparation deadlines automatically. |
| 23 | Professional Mediator Involvement | Enables qualified mediators to review case information, intervene in disputes, conduct sessions, modify workflows, communicate securely, and document outcomes. |
| 24 | Attorney Review Escalation | Routes specified legal, financial, safety, custody, or agreement issues to attorneys when automated workflows identify circumstances requiring qualified legal assessment. |
| 25 | Secure Communication | Provides encrypted participant messaging, professional communication channels, notifications, controlled document sharing, and access permissions for sensitive divorce information. |
| 26 | Audit Trails | Records important case activities, document changes, proposals, approvals, communications, access events, and decisions to support transparency and accountability. |
| 27 | E-Signature | Enables authorized participants and professionals to electronically sign eligible agreements and documents while maintaining signature records, timestamps, and consent evidence. |
| 28 | Filing Preparation | Organizes completed settlement information into jurisdiction-specific filing checklists, forms, supporting documents, signatures, and submission preparation workflows. |
| 29 | Post-Divorce Agreement Management | Provides secure access to finalized agreements, parenting plans, financial obligations, modifications, reminders, documents, and ongoing compliance-related information. |
| 30 | Human Intervention Framework | Continuously evaluates case conditions and escalates safety, legal, financial, parenting, or conflict concerns beyond automated mediation capabilities. |
