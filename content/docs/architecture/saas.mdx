## Multi-tenancy

```
Platform
│
├── Tenant
│    ├── Cases
│    ├── Users
│    ├── Professionals
│    └── Billing
│
└── Shared Services
     ├── AI Gateway
     ├── Decision Engine
     ├── Document Engine
     └── Notification Engine
```

## Potential plans

### Self-Service

AI-guided mediation.

### Assisted

AI + professional support.

### Professional

Mediator-managed cases.

### Enterprise

For:

- mediation firms
- law firms
- employee legal-benefit programs
- courts/organizations where appropriate
