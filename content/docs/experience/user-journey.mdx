---
title: User journey
description: End-to-end path from landing page through filing guidance.
---

## Step 1 — Landing page

User selects: Start My Divorce Mediation

## Step 2 — Account

Email/phone + MFA.

## Step 3 — Jurisdiction

Collect residency and case information.

## Step 4 — Eligibility

Decision-tree screening.

## Step 5 — Invite spouse

Secure invitation.

## Step 6 — Consent

Both parties accept platform terms and mediation process.

## Step 7 — Case setup

Collect:

- marriage
- children
- property
- finances
- debts

## Step 8 — Document collection

Upload evidence.

## Step 9 — AI organization

AI organizes the case.

## Step 10 — Issue identification

System identifies:

- Parenting
- Property
- Support
- Debt
- Retirement
- Taxes
- Other

## Step 11 — Individual positions

Each party privately enters positions.

## Step 12 — Shared facts

The platform identifies agreed and disputed facts.

## Step 13 — Negotiation

Issue-by-issue proposals.

## Step 14 — AI assistance

AI explains options and identifies missing information.

## Step 15 — Human escalation

Mediator reviews difficult issues.

## Step 16 — Agreement

Resolved issues are assembled.

## Step 17 — Review

Each party reviews independently.

## Step 18 — Professional/legal review

Where applicable.

## Step 19 — Signature

Electronic execution.

## Step 20 — Filing guidance

Provide jurisdiction-specific procedural information, while avoiding representations that the platform itself has completed a court filing unless that capability actually exists.
