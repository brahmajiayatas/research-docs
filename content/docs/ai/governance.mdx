## Risk management

The AI governance layer should follow established AI risk-management practices.

NIST's Generative AI Profile emphasizes managing risks throughout the AI lifecycle and identifies trustworthiness considerations including validity, reliability, safety, security, transparency, explainability, privacy and fairness.

Implement:

- AI Model Registry
- Prompt Registry
- Decision Tree Registry
- Knowledge Source Registry
- Output Policy Registry
- Evaluation Dataset
- Audit Logs
- Human Review Logs
- Incident Management

## Trace metadata

Every AI response should have internally traceable metadata:

- `case_id`
- `user_id`
- `workflow_id`
- `decision_tree_id`
- `tree_version`
- `knowledge_version`
- `model_version`
- `prompt_version`
- `timestamp`
- `risk_level`
- `escalation_status`
