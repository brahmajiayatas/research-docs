## Controlled Divorce AI

This is one of the most important parts of the product.

Do not build the product around a completely unrestricted chatbot.

Instead create a **Controlled Divorce AI**.

Architecture:

```
User
 ↓
Input Validation
 ↓
Safety Classifier
 ↓
Intent Classification
 ↓
Decision Tree
 ↓
Knowledge Retrieval
 ↓
AI Reasoning
 ↓
Policy Guardrails
 ↓
Output Validator
 ↓
User
```

The AI should operate inside a controlled domain.

## Example

User:

> "My wife is demanding $4,000 per month. What should I offer?"

The AI should not simply answer:

> "Offer $2,000."

Instead:

```
Identify issue
↓
Determine jurisdiction
↓
Collect financial inputs
↓
Determine missing information
↓
Explain relevant factors
↓
Generate possible scenarios
↓
Ask whether parties want to negotiate
↓
Generate proposal options
```

This creates an AI-assisted mediation system rather than an AI divorce lawyer.

## What "Closed AI" should mean

For this product, define Closed AI as:

### Closed knowledge domain

Only approved sources.

### Closed tools

AI can access only approved functions.

### Closed workflows

AI cannot skip required mediation stages.

### Closed outputs

Certain output types are prohibited.

### Closed data boundary

Case information is isolated from unrelated users/cases.

### Closed model gateway

All AI requests pass through your own AI gateway.

Example:

```
User
 ↓
AI Gateway
 ↓
Policy Engine
 ↓
Case Context
 ↓
Approved Knowledge
 ↓
LLM
 ↓
Output Validator
 ↓
Response
```

This approach also makes auditing considerably easier.
