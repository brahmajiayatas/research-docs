This should become the heart of the product.

## Dispute path

Every dispute follows:

```
ISSUE
 ↓
POSITION
 ↓
INTEREST
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
FACTS
 ↓
OPTIONS
 ↓
PROPOSAL
 ↓
COUNTERPROPOSAL
 ↓
NEGOTIATION
 ↓
AGREEMENT
```

## Family home dispute

Issue: Who keeps the family home?

Party A: Wants to retain home.

Party B: Wants sale.

Evidence: Mortgage + valuation + income documents.

Options:

- A. Sell property
- B. Buyout
- C. Temporary occupancy
- D. Deferred sale
- E. Professional valuation

The AI can explain the options without deciding which option the parties should choose.
