---

The AI-Assisted Divorce Mediation Platform is a secure, structured, and human-supervised digital platform designed to help couples navigate divorce and separation in a more organized, transparent, and collaborative way. The platform brings the major components of the divorce process into one guided environment—from initial onboarding and financial disclosure to parenting plans, negotiation, settlement preparation, document generation, professional review, and post-divorce agreement management.

The platform is designed primarily for low-conflict, cooperative, and uncontested divorce cases, where both spouses are willing to communicate, disclose relevant information, and work toward mutually acceptable agreements. AI assists users by organizing information, identifying unresolved issues, summarizing positions, explaining process steps, comparing proposals, generating settlement options, and preparing documents for review.

Safety and professional oversight are fundamental to the platform. Structured screening and decision trees continuously evaluate case conditions and identify situations involving significant conflict, safety concerns, coercion, complex financial matters, custody disputes, or other circumstances requiring human intervention. When appropriate, the platform escalates the case to a qualified mediator, attorney, or other professional.

The platform does not replace legal professionals or courts. Instead, it combines guided workflows, automation, AI assistance, secure collaboration, and professional oversight to make the divorce process more structured, understandable, efficient, and manageable.

The platform’s product vision is translated into the following [core capabilities](/capabilities), creating a structured end-to-end journey from initial onboarding and information gathering through AI-assisted mediation, professional escalation, settlement, filing preparation, and post-divorce management.
