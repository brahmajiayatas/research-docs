## Product vision

A useful reference point is [Divorce.ai](https://divorce.ai/how-it-works), which uses guided onboarding and structured workflows to simplify the divorce process. This product can go substantially further by combining decision trees + a controlled/closed AI layer + evidence management + negotiation workflows + human mediator escalation.

## Architectural principle

The important architectural principle should be:

**AI assists the mediation process; it does not decide the outcome, provide unauthorized legal advice, or determine what either spouse should accept.**

The system should preserve party self-determination and procedural fairness. For example, California's mediation rules emphasize balanced participation and uncoerced decisions. See [California Courts](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/three/rule3_857).
