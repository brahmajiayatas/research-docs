## Automatic escalation triggers

Create automatic escalation triggers for scenarios such as:

- threats
- coercion
- intimidation
- domestic violence disclosures
- child safety concerns
- inability to participate freely
- suspected financial concealment
- major undisclosed assets
- complex business valuation
- highly complex tax issues
- conflicting evidence
- legal representation requirements

The AI should respond conservatively and route the matter to appropriate human resources.

Do not allow the system to continue automated negotiation when a mandatory escalation condition is triggered.
