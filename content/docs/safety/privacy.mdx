## Data boundaries

```
Tenant
 ↓
Case
 ↓
Party
 ↓
Private Data
```

Use separate permissions for:

- shared case information
- Party A private notes
- Party B private notes
- mediator notes
- attorney notes
- AI-generated internal metadata

Never expose private AI conversations to the other spouse.
