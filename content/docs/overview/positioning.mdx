## Proposed product category

AI-Assisted Divorce Mediation & Settlement Platform

## Core promise

A structured, secure and AI-assisted pathway for couples to organize their divorce, understand disputed issues, negotiate proposals and prepare settlement documentation—with human professionals available when needed.

## Primary users

- Spouse A
- Spouse B
- Mediator
- Family-law professional
- Financial professional
- Platform administrator
- Compliance/security administrator

## Initial target market

Start with one jurisdiction, rather than attempting nationwide or international divorce support immediately.

For example:

**Phase 1: California**

Then build a jurisdiction framework allowing:

- California
- Texas
- New York
- Florida
- Washington
- ...

Each jurisdiction gets its own:

- legal knowledge base
- forms
- terminology
- decision-tree rules
- disclosure requirements
- workflow rules
- document templates
- escalation rules

This is much safer than building one generic "AI divorce lawyer."
